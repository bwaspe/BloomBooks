💰 Potential Margin

Cost vs. your set retail price for the current period — not actual sales data, since BloomBooks doesn't have POS integration. This shows what margin you'd realize if everything sold at the retail price you've set.

📊 Budget Baseline

Compares the selected period's spend against the historical average for that same calendar month in prior years — accounts for seasonality automatically as more history builds up.

⚠️ Stale Margins

Items where cost has moved since you last set a retail price, but the retail price hasn't been updated to match.

🌿 Stems or bunches

Nobody counts stems of limonium, gyp, statice or ruscus — the bunch is the unit. Settle a family here once and it stops being reported as a missing stem count in the holiday purchase tables. Flowers and greens only; a case of vases is a pack count, which is a different question.
